FLARE Quality model attribution

The optional Quality pack uses the FLARE contributors' model checkpoints
for the 2026 paper "Learning Visual Speech Representations via Cross-Modal
Distillation and Joint Face-Lip Modeling". The checkpoint release does not
identify its authors by name.

Source: https://huggingface.co/Anony94/FLARE-checkpoints
Revision: 6187ad650dc1786a8efc8b6bfbee37bbf62c4b2e
Checkpoint: exp_22_base/finetuned/checkpoints/checkpoint_last.pt
SHA-256: fe8c8ecab633bd4e3e490924e2bc917420b4d18d7309a5cd612902ccc842853b

The Quality pack is modified from the checkpoint: model graphs have been
exported to ONNX, with compact storage and separate beam decoder and cache
helper graphs. These exports are not newly trained model weights.
